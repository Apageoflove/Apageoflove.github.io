Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2025 · Applies to: UniDrive

UniDrive (“the App”) is a personal-use application developed by an individual developer. It runs locally on the developer’s own Windows computer and connects to cloud storage accounts that belong to the developer.

What data the App accesses

With the account owner’s explicit authorization, the App accesses files and folders in the owner’s own cloud storage accounts (Google Drive, MEGA, OneDrive, Dropbox, pCloud, Koofr, Filen) in order to display them and to upload, download, rename, move, or delete files at the owner’s direct request.

How data is used

All data accessed by the App is used solely to provide local file browsing and file management for the account owner. The App does not use data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the owner’s direct file operations.

Data collection and sharing

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. It operates entirely on the owner’s local computer. No analytics, tracking, or telemetry is included.

Data storage

File contents remain in the owner’s own cloud storage accounts and on the owner’s local disk cache. The App operates no servers that store user data.

Revoking access

The account owner may revoke the App’s access at any time via their Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Removing authorization immediately prevents the App from accessing the account.
